About the Program

What Is the Federal Support Grant?

The Federal Support Grant (FSG) is a government-administered funding initiative providing direct financial awards to qualifying U.S. residents, businesses, and organizations. Funds are non-repayable — no payback, no interest, no collateral.

  • ✓No repayment required on approved grants
  • ✓Disbursed directly to your verified bank account
  • ✓Applications reviewed within 30–45 business days
  • ✓Open to individuals, businesses, and nonprofits
  • ✓Compliant with 2 CFR federal grant regulations
  • ✓Zero application or processing fee — ever

Eligibility

Who Can Apply?

Review the requirements carefully. Ineligible applications are returned without review.

  • ✓U.S. citizens and permanent residents, 18 and above
  • ✓Registered small businesses with valid EIN
  • ✓Nonprofit organizations with 501(c)(3) status
  • ✓State, local government and tribal entities
  • ✓UK, Germany and participating EU residents
  • ✗Debarred or federally suspended individuals
  • ✗Businesses with unresolved federal tax delinquency
  • ✗Entities under active federal investigation

⚠ Fraud Alert

Protect Yourself From
Grant Scams

This program is frequently impersonated by fraudsters. Read this section carefully before taking any action related to grant funding.

The Government Will NEVER:

  • Charge a fee to process your application
  • Ask for gift cards or wire transfers
  • Request your bank login or card PIN
  • Contact you via WhatsApp or Facebook
  • Ask you to send money to receive money
  • Request a one-time password (OTP)
📞

Unsolicited Calls

We never cold-call to announce grants. Official notifications are sent only via verified government email domains.

💳

Upfront Fee Demands

Any entity requesting payment to "unlock," "release," or "process" your grant is committing federal fraud. Report them immediately.

📱

Social Media Scams

Fraudsters impersonate officials on Facebook, Instagram, and Telegram. We do not administer grants through any social media platform.

🔗

Fake Websites

Always verify the URL. This official portal uses verified GVN authentication. If the URL looks different — leave immediately.

If you have been targeted, report it to the Federal Trade Commission: reportfraud.ftc.gov
