About the Program

What Is the Federal Support Grant?

The Federal Support Grant (FSG) is an official government-administered funding initiative designed to provide financial assistance to eligible U.S. residents, small businesses, and nonprofit organizations pursuing economic development, education, housing stability, and community advancement.

Grants are non-repayable awards — recipients are not required to pay back any funds received, provided all program conditions are met and documented accurately.

  • No repayment required on approved grants
  • Applications reviewed within 30–45 business days
  • Funds disbursed directly to verified bank account
  • Available to individuals, businesses, and nonprofits
  • Fully compliant with federal grant regulations
  • No application processing fee

Program Authority

This program operates pursuant to federal funding statutes and is administered in accordance with Title 2 of the Code of Federal Regulations (2 CFR), the Federal Grant and Cooperative Agreement Act, and applicable Office of Management and Budget (OMB) guidelines.


All applicants are subject to eligibility verification, identity confirmation, and compliance review prior to award determination.

Requirements

Eligibility Criteria

Review the following criteria carefully before submitting an application. Incomplete or ineligible applications will not be processed.

✓ Who May Apply

  • U.S. citizens and permanent residents aged 18 and above
  • Registered small businesses with valid EIN
  • Nonprofit organizations with 501(c)(3) status
  • State and local government entities
  • Tribal governments and tribal organizations
  • International applicants in participating EU/UK/Germany territories
  • Educational institutions (public and accredited private)

✗ Who May Not Apply

  • Individuals currently debarred or suspended from federal programs
  • Businesses with unresolved federal tax delinquencies
  • Foreign governments and their agents
  • Applicants previously found in violation of grant terms
  • Entities engaged in lobbying activities funded by federal awards
  • Applicants under active federal investigation

Process

How to Apply

Follow the steps below in sequence. Do not skip any stage — incomplete submissions will be returned without review.

1

Confirm Eligibility

Review all eligibility requirements listed above. Ensure your intended use of funds aligns with one of the approved grant categories.

2

Prepare Required Documents

Gather your government-issued ID, Social Security Number or EIN, proof of address, recent tax returns (last 2 years), and any supporting documentation relevant to your grant category.

3

Complete the Online Application

Submit the application form below in full. Partial submissions will not be processed. Ensure all information is accurate and matches your supporting documents.

4

Application Review

Your application will be reviewed by the Grant Review Committee within 30 to 45 business days. You will receive an email notification confirming receipt and any additional information required.

5

Award Determination & Disbursement

Approved applicants will receive an official Award Letter. Funds are disbursed directly to your verified bank account within 10 business days of award confirmation.

⚠ FRAUD ALERT

Protect Yourself From Grant Scams

The Federal Support Grant program has become a target for impersonation by fraudulent actors. Please read the following carefully before taking any action related to this program.

Unsolicited Phone Calls

We never call you unexpectedly to tell you that you've been awarded a grant. All award notifications are sent via official email from verified government domains only.

Fee Demands

Legitimate government grants are always free to apply for. Any person or entity requesting payment to "process," "release," or "activate" your grant is committing fraud.

Wire Transfers & Gift Cards

The Federal Support Grant program never requests payment via wire transfer, Western Union, MoneyGram, or any form of gift card. This is a definitive scam indicator.

Social Media Impersonation

Fraudsters impersonate government officials on Facebook, Instagram, and WhatsApp. We do not administer grants through social media platforms.

The Government Will NEVER Ask You To:

  • Pay a fee to receive your grant funds
  • Provide your bank login credentials or card PIN
  • Send money via wire transfer or cryptocurrency to "unlock" your award
  • Purchase gift cards as payment for any reason
  • Share a one-time password (OTP) received on your phone
  • Contact a private individual rather than an official government office

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to repay the grant if approved? ▾
No. Approved grants are non-repayable awards. Provided you comply with all program conditions and use funds for the stated purpose, no repayment is required.
How long does the review process take? ▾
Applications are reviewed within 30 to 45 business days of receipt. Complex applications or those requiring additional documentation may take longer. You will be notified by email of any updates.
Can I apply for more than one grant category? ▾
Applicants may submit only one application per program cycle. If your project spans multiple categories, select the primary funding purpose that best describes the main use of funds.
Is there an application fee? ▾
No. The Federal Support Grant program does not charge any fee at any stage — not for application, review, processing, or disbursement. If anyone requests payment, that is fraud.
Are international applicants eligible? ▾
Eligible international applicants include residents of the United Kingdom, Germany, and other participating European Union territories, subject to their own national compliance requirements and bilateral agreements.
How will I receive my funds if approved? ▾
Funds are disbursed via direct bank transfer to the verified account provided during the award finalization stage. You will never be asked to pay anything before receiving your funds.
