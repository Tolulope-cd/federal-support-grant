Who May Apply
  • U.S. citizens & permanent residents, 18+
  • Small businesses with valid EIN
  • Nonprofits with 501(c)(3) status
  • State & local government entities
  • Tribal governments & organizations
  • UK, Germany & participating EU residents
What to Prepare
  • Government-issued photo ID
  • Social Security Number (SSN) — USA
  • EIN for business applicants
  • Proof of address
  • Last 2 years of tax returns
  • Project description / business plan
⚠ Scam Warning
  • We never charge an application fee
  • We never call you unexpectedly
  • We never ask for gift cards
  • We never use WhatsApp or Facebook
  • We never ask for your bank password
